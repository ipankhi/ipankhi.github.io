01 / About

Research at the intersection of learning and visual understanding.

I am a Ph.D. researcher at IIT Bombay focused on Computer Vision and Multimodal Learning. My research spans few-shot and contrastive learning, vision-language models, and methods for learning effectively from limited labelled data.

I have worked on applications in medical imaging and remote sensing, as well as structural health monitoring and resource-constrained robotic systems.

03 / Research experience

Research themes

01

Medical Imaging

  • Developed a Padé approximation-based loss function for long-tailed medical image classification.
  • Developed deep learning models for early detection of pre-cancerous lesions from oral mucosal images.
02

Structural Health Monitoring

  • Developed TinyML-based models for edge deployment and structural health monitoring of composite structures under thermal variations.
  • Applied unsupervised learning for anomaly detection using ultrasonic guided-wave data.
03

Remote Sensing

  • Developed spatially-aware cross-modal contrastive learning for low-shot hyperspectral image classification.
  • Worked on bi-modal textual prompt learning for vision-language models in remote sensing.
04

Robotics & Embedded Learning

  • Developed a framework for trajectory estimation and navigation of a robotic sensor platform with a resource-constrained embedded controller.
  • Developed monocular depth estimation for quadruped robot navigation.
